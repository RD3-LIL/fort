Emberhold

A Stronghold 2 style pixel lordship: run the economy, feed your people well, earn honour, keep crime down, and hold the walls when raiders come.

Spring, Year 1
Pick a building on the right, then click a green tile to place it. Click any building to see what it does.

Woodcutters work best beside woods. Quarries must sit next to grey stone. Farms like open grass. Walls and towers are your raid defence, guard posts fight crime and raiders both.

      Crime & punishment

      Crime climbs when folk are hungry, overtaxed or idle. Guard posts catch thieves, the stocks make an example of them, and a caught thief sometimes raises a cheer.

      The garrison

      Outlying villages swear to your banner for honour, then send carts of goods to your stockpile every few days. Upgrade an estate and its carts grow heavier.

      Honour: 0

      Honour is earned by good rule, not bought. A staffed church grants honour each month while popularity stays above 60. Feasts are the fast road.
